# P5 SHIP — polish, performance, bonus levels, handoff

**Dates:** 28–29 November. The game freezes on 29 November at 18:00 owner's time (UTC+5). Check the clock with `date`.

**Owner feedback on P4** may be in the message above this prompt. Apply it first.

**Read now:**

- `docs/QUALITY.md` §4, §6 and §8;
- `docs/MINDBLOWN.md` §5–7;
- `docs/DESIGN.md` §9–10;
- `NOTES.md`.

## Must, in this order

1. **First impression.** Title, level intros, the checkout close-up, the receipt and medal moments, the first 30 seconds of level 1. Every second of `DESIGN.md` §9 should look and sound as good as a trailer.
2. **Juice:**
   - stickers, impact frames, camera kicks, spill fountains, crowd reactions, the voice mix;
   - every action has a sound; each store has its music, with the Madness build-up and CLOSING TIME.
3. **Performance and devices:**
   - check the quality tiers against `QUALITY.md` §6. Force MED and LOW and check they look intentional. Ask the owner in the final report to try a laptop with integrated graphics if one is available;
   - touch controls and the phone layout;
   - pause on focus loss; window resize.
4. **Jury review** (`QUALITY.md` §4) and its fixes.
5. **Release.** Close the release checklist (`QUALITY.md` §8) and build the handoff (`MINDBLOWN.md` §6), checked on a clean copy.

## Should, only with time

Levels 7 NEON NIGHTS and 8 LAST CALL (`LEVELS.md` §5), with their kits.

- Start one only when every must item is done and at least 10 hours remain before the freeze.
- A bonus level ships only if it passes `LEVELS.md` §6 and `ART.md` §9 point 4. Otherwise leave it out of the build (git keeps it).
- If one ships, repeat must item 5.

## Done when

- The release checklist is closed.
- The handoff package is built and checked.
- The release is tagged `p5` and `release`.

The final report lists:

- what shipped;
- the 3 known issues that remain;
- the exact steps for the owner to upload the game to Mindblown.

Do not upload or publish anything yourself (`MINDBLOWN.md` §7).
