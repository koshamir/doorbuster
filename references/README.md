# Visual references

Two frames from Hi-Fi Rush, kept here only as a rendering reference. Never ship them, and never copy their characters, logos or UI.

| File | What it shows | What to take |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `reference_1.jpg` | gameplay | ink silhouettes; two-band cel shading with hard terminators; bold graphic floor markings; saturated accents on a calm environment; comic stickers and numbers |
| `reference_2.png` | key art of the cast | faces with big eyes, highlights and strong brows; chunky hair clumps with flat highlight shapes; colour-blocked clothes with drawn seams; strong silhouette variety |

What to take and what to leave is defined in `docs/ART.md` §1. Our characters are chunky toy figures, not realistic proportions.
